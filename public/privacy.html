Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. What information is collected

To generate a rewrite, you provide text directly into the tool. This content is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response, and is not stored on our servers beyond what's needed to complete that request.

2. Access codes

Your access code and unlock status are stored locally in your browser (via localStorage), not on a central server tied to your identity.

3. Payments

Payments are processed by a third-party payment provider (Lava.top). We do not directly collect or store your card details.

4. What is not collected

SayItRight AI does not use tracking cookies, third-party ad trackers, or behavioral analytics.

5. Your rights

You can ask what data, if any, is associated with you, and request it be deleted, by writing to kssw117@gmail.com.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to kssw117@gmail.com.
