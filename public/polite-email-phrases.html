FAQ

How do I sound polite in English emails?Use questions instead of commands (“Could you…?”), soften with “I think” or “just”, and focus on the problem, not the person.

Is “ASAP” rude?Not always, but on its own it can sound demanding. A specific time with a reason (“by 3 pm, so I can…”) is clearer and friendlier.

Why do my messages sound rude when I don't mean it?Direct phrasing that is normal in many languages can sound harsh in English. Small softeners make a big difference.
